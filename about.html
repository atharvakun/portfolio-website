Company Overview

A single-roof partner for precision manufacturing.

Supplar Engineering Pvt. Ltd. is a precision manufacturing partner built around three integrated disciplines — fine wire winding, wiring harness assembly and injection moulding — serving the automotive, two-wheeler, appliance and industrial sectors.

Operating from our ISO 9001:2015 certified facility in Chakan, Pune — one of India's principal automotive manufacturing hubs — each division is run with its own dedicated machinery, process discipline and in-house testing.

Customers can engage us for a single discipline or for a fully integrated, single-roof solution — with the same rigorous quality system and delivery discipline applied across all three divisions.
