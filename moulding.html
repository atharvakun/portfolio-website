Division 03 · Our newest manufacturing vertical

Injection Moulding.

Supplar has expanded into precision plastic injection moulding — extending our single-roof capability from wound components and wiring harnesses into fully moulded, engineered parts: connector housings, bushes, grommets and structural components, under the same quality system our customers already rely on.

In-house, end to end

Moulded from material to inspected part.

Horizontal and vertical presses run engineering-grade thermoplastics — nylon, PBT, ABS and PP — into connector housings, bushes, grommets and structural parts, trimmed and inspected under the same ISO 9001:2015 system.
