Winding Machinery

Servo accuracy, automated.

Automatic multi-spindle coil winding machines produce linear-wound coils — relay, solenoid, rotor and contactor coils, ferrite-core transformers and ignition coils.

Multi-axis movement executes terminal wrapping, wire routing and wire gripping & cutting automatically, combining mechanical, pneumatic and electronic systems for high, repeatable performance.

  • AC Servo AxesPrecise multi-axis motion for repeatable winding accuracy across the full gauge range.
  • Automatic TwistingFully automatic rebound-structure twister for intricate winding patterns.
  • Wide Coil TypesRelay, solenoid, rotor and contactor coils, ferrite-core transformers, ignition coils.
